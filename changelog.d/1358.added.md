**`maintain coverage` lists a native semantic layer's declarations.** Under `semantic.vendor` set to Ossie, SLayer, Cube, Databricks or Snowflake, the declarations section adds the layer's own facts beside the dbt manifest's, and every record names its `source`. The keys and joins the layer declares are the ones `maintain grain` re-verifies, so they read `dex_verify` with `declared_grain_not_unique`, `declared_join_orphans` and, for a join declared `many_to_one` or `one_to_one`, `join_cardinality_exceeded`. What the layer's `dex` metadata states (roles, references, population, unit, additivity) reads `none`, since nothing checks it on that layer. A fact a dbt model and the layer both declare on the same relation, spelled alike, is listed once per source with `also_declared_by` and counted once. Still free and connectionless, and no population value or rule text is shown.
