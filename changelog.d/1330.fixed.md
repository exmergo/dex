**`maintain verify` now reports `runtime error`, `failed`, `partial success` and other non-success dbt node statuses instead of silently omitting them.** Build-status findings, uniqueness-test grain proofs, and snapshot execution checks in temporal contracts now share `transform.evidence`'s allow-list status classification: known failure statuses raise `node_failed` and trace skipped descendants (including skips caused by a failed test on a parent model) back to the failing ancestor, `severity: warn` continues to raise `node_warned`, and `partial success` or unrecognized statuses raise `node_incomplete` (`medium`) rather than passing silently or being mislabeled as failures.
