**Governed Metric View queries are held to the reviewed file, and `explore semantic list --deployed` reads the deployed views on Databricks.** Under `semantic.vendor: databricks`, before a governed `query` or `values` is priced, dex reads the deployed Metric View back from Unity Catalog (`tables.get`, no warehouse) and compares everything the query relies on with the repository's file: the `source` and `filter`, each named field and measure and every member it reads, a grained field's `format`, and the joins those reach. A difference refuses the query, naming the member and the field; reserialized YAML, whitespace and identifier case are not differences. `list --deployed` lists the views deployed from the configured files, with per-view coverage (`full`, `limited` or `refused`), so a view this identity can browse but not read back is named rather than missing. A plain `list` never contacts Databricks. The coverage shape and the read-back refusals are now shared with Snowflake.
