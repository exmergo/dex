**The query firewall holds the columns of Elementary's schema that carry source rows.** `test_result_rows.result_row` and `result_rows` on `elementary_test_results` and its alert views hold failing rows of tests, serialized, and are held at the blocking threshold whatever the cache says (a measuring aggregate over them runs). `dimension_value` carries the PII flags of the columns the reading SELECT bounds it to with `WHERE dimension = '<column>'` (or `IN (...)`), and is held without that bound, since rows a removed monitoring test wrote stay in the table. A relation in an `elementary` or `*_elementary` schema that is none of the package's models, such as a test result `tests_use_temp_tables` leaves there, is refused as a whole. `explore profile` records no minimum, maximum or value domain for any of them, and a `pii_overrides` entry does not clear them (#1310).
