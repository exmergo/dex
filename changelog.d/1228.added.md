**A model with no declared `population.parent` is measured against a drafted one.** `transform plan` drafts the entity each model is about (`data.declarations.<model>.drafted.population_parent`): the model, seed or snapshot whose tested, declared or profiled unique key is the model's grain, else the parent a `relationships` test, `references` or clean verified join names, else the "one row per" noun of its description, else its driving relation. It is labelled `drafted` with its evidence, never written, and a declared parent always wins. `transform build --verify` measures the built model against it on the relation dbt wrote, so an aggregate CTE joined to its parent is no longer a blind spot: `data.verification.population_delta` counts orphan keys, missing parents and parents kept with no children, and a non-trivial count is `needs_decision`, a `population_delta` finding and an entry under `data.completion.obligations.needs_decision`, settled by declaring the parent or by an assumption naming the key at `request`, `contract` or `precedent` tier. Counts and key column names only. In `data.change_scope`, re-driving a model (a new driving relation, or a declared parent removed or replaced, against the applied model or an earlier unapplied plan) is a `redriven` change that only a `request` or `contract` assumption settles, and the procedure now routes NULL or zero aggregates on a parent-driven model to the join key rather than to a new driving relation (#1228).
