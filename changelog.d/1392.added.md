**`explore semantic query` and `values` run on Databricks Metric Views.** Under `semantic.vendor: databricks`, dex renders `SELECT ... MEASURE(...) FROM <deployed view> GROUP BY ALL` from the repository's Metric View files and executes it through its own Databricks connector, priced from the base tables the request reaches and run under `STATEMENT_TIMEOUT`. Tokens are `view.field` and `view.measure`; a grain suffix buckets a field formatted `date` or `date_time`, and `--where` is a Databricks predicate over the view's fields. The PII gate screens every grouped and filtered field and every column a value-returning measure reads. Window and `partition` measures, views declaring `parameters` or a `materialization`, a measure in `--where`, two views in one query and any other connector are refused by name before anything is sent. The deployed view is found by its file's stem among the Metric Views the source allowlist holds; its definition is not yet compared with the file, and every result says so.
