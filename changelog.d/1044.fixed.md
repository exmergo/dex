**verify row population for incremental models rebuilt with `--full-refresh`.**
`transform build --verify` previously skipped incremental models that it had just
rebuilt under `--full-refresh`, because verification relied solely on the manifest's
persisted `full_refresh` configuration rather than tracking which models were
actually rebuilt in full during the current run. Verification now accepts the
execution summary's rebuilt models and verifies their row population while
preserving the skip for models that were not refreshed.
