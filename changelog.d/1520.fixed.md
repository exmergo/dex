**A free local connector no longer warns that the confirm handshake was skipped.** Every command that would ask for cost confirmation carried the "no confirm handshake" warning on a connector that cannot bill, so it sat at the top of `warnings` in 41 of 41 `transform build` envelopes in one DuckDB project, beside findings that do need action, and readers learned to skip the list. The warning is gone on `free_local`; the information is kept as a field instead, `cost.handshake: "not_applicable"`, with `"confirmed"` where the caller confirmed on a billed connector and `"required"` where a billed paradigm still needs the confirmation (the refusal carries that cost). A billed connector's handshake still gates spend exactly as before (#1520).
