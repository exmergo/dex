**`explore inventory --rank` and `explore map` leave the Elementary package's own tables out of the ranking.** In a warehouse where a project installs `elementary-data/elementary`, the package's metadata tables (`dbt_run_results`, `data_monitoring_metrics`, `elementary_test_results` and the rest), written on every run, could outrank the project's own tables on size, and `explore map` spent its profiling scans on them. They are now recognised from the warehouse by their signature (a namespace holding at least three of the package's table names, one of them a name only the package uses) and left out by default: not scored, not profiled by `map` (`--full` included), and not described in its `data.objects`. Only the package's tables are left out, so a project that builds the package into its own schema keeps its own tables ranked. `data.excluded` names them (`count`, `reason: elementary`, `by_namespace`, `objects`) and a note says so. They stay readable: an unranked `explore inventory` lists them, `explore profile` and `explore query` read them as any other table, and `--include-elementary` ranks them with the rest (#1311).
