**Governed `explore semantic query` and `values` on Snowflake Semantic Views, and `explore semantic list --deployed`.** Under `semantic.vendor: snowflake`, Dex renders `SELECT * FROM SEMANTIC_VIEW(<deployed view> DIMENSIONS ... METRICS ... [WHERE ...])` from the repository's catalog and runs it through its own Snowflake connector under the cost guard (`execution: dex`); every other connector is refused by name. Tokens are the catalog's (`sales.orders.revenue`, `sales.revenue_per_customer`), one view per query, and result columns carry them. A grain-suffixed time dimension (`sales.orders.order_date__month`) renders as `DATE_TRUNC`, `--where` is a Snowflake predicate over dimensions and facts, and a query of facts alone takes `--group-by <fact>` and no metric. Refused by name before anything is sent: facts beside metrics or dimensions, a private, window, level-of-detail or `using_relationships` member, a view declaring `variables`, a dimension the metric's table does not reach many-to-one, a grain on a categorical dimension, a bare `--grain`, and a `--where` naming a metric or one Dex cannot read. The PII gate screens grouped and filtered members and the inputs of a metric that returns one of its values, through the catalog's lineage. Before the cost handshake, Dex locates the deployed view with a warehouse-free `SHOW SEMANTIC VIEWS` over the source allowlist, reads it back with `SYSTEM$READ_YAML_FROM_SEMANTIC_VIEW` (which resumes no warehouse), and refuses the query when anything it relies on differs from the repository's file, naming the member and the field; a view defined only in the repository, or in two allowlisted schemas, is refused by name. The statement is priced from the base tables the members reach, since Snowflake keeps no bytes for a semantic view. `explore semantic list --deployed` reads the deployed views instead of the files, with `source: deployed` and per-view `coverage` (`full`, `limited`, `refused`); a plain `list` never contacts Snowflake.
