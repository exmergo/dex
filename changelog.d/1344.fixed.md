**SLayer authoring and retype checks resolve measure references read through joins.** Saved measures that aggregate a column across single-hop or multi-hop joins now evaluate that column's allowed aggregation rules, including primary key restrictions and type defaults for ARRAY and TEXT columns. When a joined column is retyped, maintain schema lists affected dependent measures under `breaks_measures` and advises on the required measure rewrites. Unknown or unresolvable joins remain explicitly unchecked during authoring validation.
