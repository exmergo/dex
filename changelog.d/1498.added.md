**`maintain coverage` matches a declared fact to the tests that carry its claim.** A test Dex rendered names the fact it checks in `config.meta.dex.claim` (#1448); the declarations section now matches it to that fact by the claim, whatever its kind or column, and shows `claim` on the test's entry in the resource's `tests` list. A test with no claim, hand-written or rendered before #1448, is matched by kind and column as before, and each fact says which under `matched_by`. A test whose claim names no fact the declaration still states, a stale render or a test of a model or column renamed since, is listed under `declarations.stale_claims`; it still counts where its kind and column match, so a rename keeps its coverage. Remove the test and declare the fact again to render it with a current claim (#1498).
