Dex gives your coding agent a repeatable way to understand a warehouse, change
its dbt project, and check that the results are right. You keep your existing
project; Dex adds profiling, reviewable plans, dev builds, and drift checks.

## How it fits

Run Dex from your project directory. Your agent drives the commands and reads
their JSON results. The same commands work directly in a terminal.

Exploration reads your source data. Authoring produces plans for your project
files; applying a plan writes those files, and a build writes to a dev target.
Your repository remains the source of truth.

Cost checks and PII handling live in the engine. Before using a warehouse of your
own, follow [Connect your data](/dex/connect) to set the scope and spending limits.
For the precise boundaries, including the hosted dbt semantic-layer exception,
read [cost controls](/dex/guardrails/cost-controls) and the
[PII policy](/dex/guardrails/pii-policy).
